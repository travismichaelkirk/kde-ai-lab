# KDE AI Lab v0.7 — Tasker Diagnostic Workbench

## Purpose

Provide repeatable Fedora-based diagnostic tools for investigating
Android and Tasker behavior through ADB.

The workbench follows the KDE AI Lab development philosophy:
automate mechanical work while preserving deliberate verification.

## Scope

The v0.7 workbench includes three utilities:

1. Android device status inspection.
2. Tasker live log monitoring.
3. Timestamped Android screenshot capture.

These utilities are intended for observation and troubleshooting.
They do not modify Tasker tasks, profiles, or Android settings.

## Android Device Status

Script: scripts/diagnostics/android-status.sh

Run: scripts/diagnostics/android-status.sh

Checks ADB availability, device authorization, device model,
Android version, API level, and ADB connectivity.

This utility does not modify Android settings.

## Tasker Live Log Monitor

Script: scripts/diagnostics/tasker-log-monitor.sh

Default monitoring:
scripts/diagnostics/tasker-log-monitor.sh

Warnings-only monitoring:
scripts/diagnostics/tasker-log-monitor.sh --warnings

Displays live Tasker-related Android logcat output.
Press Ctrl+C to stop monitoring.

Both monitoring modes were tested successfully.

## Android Screenshot Capture

Script: scripts/diagnostics/android-screenshot.sh

Run: scripts/diagnostics/android-screenshot.sh

Captures the Android display as a timestamped PNG file.
Screenshots are stored locally in the captures/ directory.

Screenshot permissions: 600 (owner read/write only).
The captures/ directory is excluded from Git.

Screenshot capture was successfully tested and validated.

## Security and Privacy

- Utilities operate through an authorized ADB connection.
- Diagnostic scripts do not modify Tasker tasks or profiles.
- Screenshot captures remain local and are excluded from Git.
- Screenshots and log output may contain sensitive information.
- Android work-profile security boundaries must be respected.
- Diagnostic output should be reviewed before sharing.

## Known Limitations

- ADB must be installed and the Android device authorized.
- Utilities expect exactly one authorized Android device.
- Wireless ADB connectivity may need to be reestablished.
- Logcat warnings do not necessarily identify root causes.
- Android may restrict screenshots of protected content.
- The utilities do not automatically repair detected problems.

## Operating Policy

- Observe and verify before changing Android configurations.
- Keep diagnostic captures out of Git.
- Respect Android work-profile security boundaries.
- Preserve working Tasker automations unless changes are justified and tested.
- Commit and tag only verified known-good milestones.
